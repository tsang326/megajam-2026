# Deep Cut – game outline (user-approved frame, 2026-11-13)

**Theme reading:** "the idea you shelved… finally build it". The player *is* the shelved idea, sees from second one what was cut, spends the game restoring it, and the ending shows the restored result.

**One line:** Unit 0, a diving-robot prototype shelved at 2 AM, wakes on the sea floor missing most of its features, and rebuilds itself – and the deleted footage of the mission that replaced it – one cut at a time.

## Opening (≤ 15 s, skippable after first play)
1. Design file card: `UNIT 0 – PROTOTYPE · SHELVED 02:00 AM · reason: over budget`. Feature list with most lines struck out: ~~HEADLAMP~~ ~~GRIPPER~~ ~~MULTI-CAM~~.
2. Fathom Dynamics official mission film: "Exploration mission successful. Unit 7 defective, decommissioned." The reel timeline shows black gaps `[SCENE REMOVED]`.
3. Control. The cut reel and the struck-out feature list stay visible in the HUD all game.

**Upgrades are physical parts (user, 2026-11-13):** each fills an empty spot on Unit 0's body and makes it visibly bigger and more complete: headlamp in the forehead slot, gripper gauntlet on the capped right forearm, camera ear-pods on the helmet. Three features for three stages; stage 4 adds no feature. In the ending Unit 0 appears fully armoured (the Director's Cut look) as the reward for a continuous reel.

## Core loop
Move by match cut between survey-camera shots → in a shot, solve an *edit* puzzle to reveal the tape marks → cut → find a part → **FEATURE CUT → BUILT** (a struck-out line on the design file is rebuilt) and restore-by-cut fills one gap on the reel → the restored seconds change what the old footage meant.

Edit puzzles only (no valves/levers/doors): carry an object across the cut (B), choose which camera to cut to (A), cut on the right moment (something drifting, a current turning), cut an object out (pick the shot where it isn't), and lamp-reveal (the lamp is a rebuilt feature).

## Stages (each restores one feature + one gap)
| Stage | Feature rebuilt | New puzzle verb | Restored footage reveals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HEADLAMP | lamp shows hidden marks; first carry (B) | diver head-cam: "the monster attack" – but the first restored seconds show a shape that does not match the gentle creature |
| 2 | GRIPPER | carry objects / a rescued crew bot across cuts (B) | the attacker was a second, unknown predator; Fathom cut it out to blame the gentle creature and justify capturing it |
| 3 | MULTI-CAM | choose camera (A), routes split | Unit 7 cut scenes from the record to hide the gentle creature and cover the crew; it took the blame ("defective") |
| 4 | (none – finale) | cut the predator out of the film: the carry rule works on it too – cut while it is outside Unit 0's carry radius and it stays in the old shot; inside the radius it comes along and catches you (static, TAKE 2). MULTI-CAM shows where it comes from; gripper and lamp are used along the way. Dorsal fin glows as it nears (feedback only) | Unit 7 was built from Unit 0's shelved features; the parts Unit 0 collects are its own |

Crew: rescue the other units along the way (count shown as CREW x/n).

**Predator (user, 2026-11-13):** appears in play as a shadow (eyes + sound) only in shots whose puzzle is already solved (marks visible). The only escape is to cut away. Caught = static, then TAKE 2 of that shot. No blood, no combat.

## Ending
Unit 0 complete, Unit 7 and crew found, reel continuous. **Final cut on a timeline (user, 2026-11-13):** the player keeps or drops each restored scene on the reel, then the game plays exactly the cut they made, next to the official cut from the opening. Fallback if time runs short: play the full restored cut side by side with the official cut.

Twist C (user, 2026-11-13): Unit 7 was built from Unit 0's shelved features; the parts Unit 0 collects are its own.
